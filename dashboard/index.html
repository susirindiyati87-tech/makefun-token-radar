How the value is built

No name is ever special-cased. Every row is scored by the same three figures from the snapshot: its TVL, its seven-day growth, and how long it has run with no token.

  1. Category multiple. The median market-cap-to-TVL ratio of the tokenised protocols in the same category, when the snapshot records at least three of them; otherwise the whole snapshot's median. Clamped to 0.05 – 1.00. A lending protocol is valued like lending protocols trade; a DEX like DEXes trade.
  2. Base. TVL × category multiple.
  3. Growth. Seven-day TVL change, clamped to −60% … +120%, times a weight of 0.5, added to 1.
  4. Maturity. 0.6 + (age in days, capped at 1,095) ÷ 1,095 × 0.4. A protocol with no listing date counts as 365 days old.
  5. Implied value. Base × growth × maturity, rounded to whole dollars, at least $1.

What the board leaves out

Versioned pools (a name ending in V2 or 2.0), wrapped, staked and bridged products, and exchanges are not ranked: none of them is a protocol still waiting on a launch of its own. The watchlist is the floor, never the ceiling — the board also carries pre-token protocols below the $50,000,000 floor that meet the same definition.
